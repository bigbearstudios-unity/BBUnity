# Changelog

All notable changes to this project will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- `CombatTag` (Create > BBUnity > Combat > Combat Tag): a label asset an `Attack` or `CombatAction` can carry. Tags travel with every hit as `HitInfo.Tags` / `HitInfo.HasTag(tag)` so the defender can react to the kind of hit. BBUnity gives no tag any meaning. `Attack` has a new Tags list.
- `AttributeBasedCombatController.HitInterceptor`: a `Func<HitInfo, bool>` a game sets to take over a hit. When it returns true `ApplyHit` stops there (no `OnHit`, no mitigation, no damage) and `OnHitIntercepted` fires instead.
- Hit effects: a `HitEffect` (abstract, picked from an "Add Effect" dropdown) plays when a hit lands, with `SpawnEffect` (a pooled prefab at the point of contact, flipped to the direction of the hit) as the first. An `Attack` or `CombatAction` has Hit Effects that always play at the point of contact. A combatant has Surface Tags (what it is made of) and a Receiver Hit Effects table of rows `{ attack tags, surface tags } → effects`, where the most specific matching row plays. Both sit directly on `AttributeBasedCombatController` or in a `CombatantDefinition` (the controller's own surface tags add to the definition's, and its table row wins a tie). `HitInfo` now carries the contact point (`Point`, `HasPoint`, `WithPoint`) and the action's effects; an intercepted hit plays no effects.
- More hit effects: `TimeScaleEffect` (hit-stop through the `TimeController`), `ScreenShakeEffect` (through `CameraShaker.Current`, along the hit or random) and `SoundEffect`.
- Sound: `Sound` (clips picked at random, volume and pitch with variation, mixer group, spatial blend, max simultaneous copies), `SoundDefinition` (Create > BBUnity > Audio > Sound Definition) and `SoundReference` (inline, or a shared definition). `AudioController` is a fixed pool of voices on the `GameplayController` object (added automatically, `GameplayController.AudioController`) that every sound plays through, so a sound is never cut off when what triggered it is pooled or destroyed. `SoundController` plays named sounds from Animation Events (`PlaySound`).
- Camera shake: abstract `CameraShaker` (`CameraShaker.Current`) and `CinemachineCameraShaker`, which generates a Cinemachine impulse (needs a `CinemachineImpulseSource` beside it and an impulse listener on the camera). It lives in its own `Unity.BBUnity.Cinemachine` assembly that only compiles when Cinemachine is installed.
- `CombatAction` (Create > BBUnity > Combat > Combat Action): an asset holding damage entries, tags and a cooldown in milliseconds, for a game to subclass. `AttributeBasedCombatController.CreateHit(action)` resolves it like an Attack, and `IsReady(action)` / `TryUse(action)` track its cooldown.
- `ScreenFader` (Runtime/Gameplay/Controllers): the one full-screen fade overlay, added to the `GameplayController`'s object automatically and reached via `GameplayController.ScreenFader`. `LevelTransitionController` now fades through it instead of drawing its own overlay.
- `EnemySpawnNode` now spawns its Enemy when the level starts (into the node's own scene) and rests it on the ground below the node, found by a downward ray against its `GroundLayers`. It is left at the node, with a warning, if no ground is found.
- `KeepUnflipped` (Utilities): keeps a child, such as a stat bar, reading the right way round when its parent is flipped by a negative scale.
- `LevelTransitionController` (Runtime/Gameplay/Controllers): covers the screen while `GameplayController.TransitionToLevel` swaps levels, when one is in the scene. Transition Type is `Fade` (Fade Color, Fade Out Time and Fade In Time in milliseconds) or `Loading Screen` (placeholder, cuts instantly for now). `MinimumTransitionTime` (ms) keeps the screen covered for at least that long, so a fast load doesn't flash past. `StopTimeOnTransition` (default on) holds game time at zero, through the `TimeController`, from the start of the transition until the fade-in begins. `TransitionCooldown` (ms, default 500) now lives here rather than on `GameplayController`; without a controller in the scene the default is used.
- `AttributeEffector` system for `DynamicIntegerAttribute`: `RegenerationEffector` (amount per interval, with a delay after the attribute is spent/damaged; negative drains) and `MaximumModifierEffector` (flat or percent of base, increase or decrease). Authored in the Inspector with an "Add Effector" picker, or added at runtime with `AddEffector(effector, duration, source)` / `RemoveEffector` / `RemoveEffectorsFrom`.
- `DynamicIntegerAttribute`: `BaseMaximum` (authored) alongside `Maximum` (after effectors), `Current` clamped to `[Minimum, Maximum]`, `CanAfford`/`TrySpend`, `SetBaseMaximum`; `onChange` also fires when the maximum changes.
- `EntityAttribute.Name`, `AttributeController.Get(string)` and `Get<TValue>()`, and public `GetAttribute<T>()`; `AttributeBasedCombatController.Mana`.
- `NamedStaticIntegerAttribute` / `NamedDynamicIntegerAttribute`: generic, designer-named attributes (several per controller), with `StaticIntegerAttribute` as the effector-free data type.
- `CombatantDefinition` asset (Create > BBUnity > Combat > Combatant Definition) holding shared attributes and reductions, assigned in `AttributeBasedCombatController`'s new Definition field. The controller works on its own copy; anything set directly on the controller overrides the definition (attributes by Name, reductions per attribute).
- `TimeController` (Runtime/Gameplay/Controllers): timed, optionally cumulative `Time.timeScale` modifiers that always return to normal speed once they expire. Added to the `GameplayController` automatically and reached via `GameplayController.TimeController`.
- `AttributeBar` (Runtime/Gameplay/Controllers): a HUD slider that binds to a Player attribute at runtime via `PlayerController.Current`, so a scene-based HUD can show a prefab-spawned Player.
- `EntityAttribute.ValueChanged`, `MinimumValue` and `MaximumValue`: generic, type-agnostic access to an attribute's value changes and range.

- `LevelTransitionNode` now does something: a Player entering its trigger collider makes `GameplayController.TransitionToLevel` load the target scene additively, move the Player to the target `PlayerSpawnNode`, make it the active scene and unload the scene the node was in. `Level` places the Player at its default spawn node when a level is entered directly. `PlayerController.TeleportTo` / `PlayerMovementController.Teleport` move the Player with no leftover momentum.

### Changed
- **Breaking:** the assemblies are split. New: `Unity.BBUnity.Foundation` (empty for now), `Unity.BBUnity.2D` (`SpriteAnimator`, `Tiles`, `Entity2D`, `PlatformingPlayerMovementController` with its movement state, `AttackCollider2D`, the 2D component settings) and `Unity.BBUnity.Platformer` (the platformer generation, which replaces `Unity.BBUnity.Generation.Platformer`). Editor code for 2D is in `Unity.BBUnity.2D.Editor`. Core no longer references 2D or Platformer, so a project that uses them adds `Unity.BBUnity.2D` or `Unity.BBUnity.Platformer` to its asmdef references. Namespaces are unchanged except `BBUnity.Generation.Platformer.*`, which is now `BBUnity.Platformer.Generation.*` (editor: `BBUnity.Editor.Platformer.Generation`), and its asset menu path follows.
- `LevelBuilder.Build` takes an `ILevelLayerBuilder` instead of a `TilePaletteDefinition`. Pass `new TilemapLevelPainter(palette)` from `BBUnity.Tiles` to get the tilemaps as before.
- **Breaking:** the `Gameplay.` namespace prefix is gone. Types moved to feature namespaces (`BBUnity.Statistics`, `BBUnity.Combat`, `BBUnity.Input`, `BBUnity.Movement`, `BBUnity.Entities`, `BBUnity.Audio`, `BBUnity.World`, `BBUnity.Spawning`, `BBUnity.GameFlow`, `BBUnity.UI`, `BBUnity.Tags`, `BBUnity.Magnitudes`, `BBUnity.CameraSystem`, and `BBUnity.Controllers` for `SessionController`, `PlayerController` and `TimeController`), and the folders follow. Editor code moved to the matching `BBUnity.Editor.*` namespaces. Menu paths follow the new namespaces. MonoBehaviours and ScriptableObjects keep working by script GUID; `[SerializeReference]` entries and component type names stored as text in assets show as missing and must be rebuilt. `IElementInstantiator` and `RuntimeElementInstantiator` are now in separate files.
- `GameplayController` renamed `SessionController` (and the game's `VampireEmpireGameplayController` to `VampireEmpireSessionController`); `SessionController.Current` replaces `GameplayController.Current`. Script GUIDs unchanged, so existing scenes keep working.
- **Breaking:** every authored duration and public time parameter is now whole milliseconds (`int`) instead of float seconds: `TimeController.RequestTimeScale` duration, `DynamicIntegerAttribute.AddEffector` duration, `RegenerationEffector` interval and delay (`SecondsSinceDecrease` is now `MillisecondsSinceDecrease`), `Poise` regeneration time, `CoyoteTime` threshold, `PlayerMovementController.TogglePlatformCollision`, `UIScreen` fade duration, `FloatingTextController` lifetime and fade, `LevelTransitionController` transition cooldown and `TestUtilities.WaitForSeconds` (now `WaitForMilliseconds`). Per-frame deltas (`Update(deltaTime)`, `Tick`, `TimeController.Advance`) stay in seconds, as Unity supplies them.
- `PlayerTransitionNode` renamed `LevelTransitionNode` (menu `BBUnity/Levels/Level Transition Node`). Script GUID unchanged, so existing scenes keep working.
- `BootSettings`: `BootScene` → `OnBootScene` and `FrontendScene` → `MainMenuScene` (`GameplayScene` unchanged). Serialized values carry over via `FormerlySerializedAs`.
- `BBUnitySettings`: the entity-building options are now grouped in an `Entities` section (`EntitySettings`). `AdditionalComponentTypes` moved to `settings.Entities.AdditionalComponentTypes`; the existing project asset was migrated.
- The level building blocks — `Level`, `PlayerSpawnNode`, `EnemySpawnNode`, `LevelTransitionNode` and the `Enemy` marker, with their inspectors and `SceneComponentScanner` — moved into BBUnity (`BBUnity.Gameplay.Levels`) from the game project. Script GUIDs are unchanged, so scenes and prefabs keep working. They appear under `BBUnity/Levels/` in the Add Component menu.
- Add Component menu: `AttributeBasedCombatController` and `PlayerMovementController` are under `BBUnity/Entities/Controllers/`, `TimeController` under `BBUnity/Controllers/`.
- Every concrete BBUnity component now has an Add Component menu entry under `BBUnity/` using its namespace as the folder path and its class name (spaced) as the label, instead of appearing under `Scripts` with its full namespaced name. `StateController`, `Entity2D`/`Entity3D`, `SpriteAnimator` and `UIBackgroundGradient` moved to match their namespace.

- `InputState` is now `internal` (the test assembly sees it through `InternalsVisibleTo`).
- Removed `using` directives that pointed at the game's own namespaces from package files; the package cannot reference game code.
- Editor tools reorganized by purpose: windows live in `BBUnity.Editor.Windows` (`AnimationEventAuditorWindow`), and the statistics and combat drawers/editors are in `BBUnity.Editor.Gameplay.Entities.Controllers.Statistics` / `.Combat`. Drawers and editors otherwise stay beside the namespace of the type they draw.
- All custom inspectors, property drawers and the Animation Event Auditor window are now UI Toolkit (`CreateInspectorGUI` / `CreatePropertyGUI` / `CreateGUI`); no IMGUI remains in the Editor assembly. Shared building blocks live in `BBUnity.Editor.Toolkit`: `PickerField` (labeled dropdown button), `ArrayListElement` (boxed rows with move/remove), `ManagedReferenceField` (type picker for a single `[SerializeReference]` slot) and `InspectorElements`. Reordering lists uses move up/down buttons rather than drag handles. The reflection into Unity's internal `EditorGUI.SortingLayerField` is gone; the sorting layer drawer is a plain dropdown over `SortingLayer.layers`.
- New: `Window > BBUnity > Settings` (`BBUnitySettingsWindow`) edits the project's `BBUnitySettings`, or offers a *Create Settings* button that creates `Assets/Resources/BBUnitySettings.asset` when there is none. **Breaking:** the Animation Event Auditor menu moved from `Window/BBUnity/Animation Event Auditor` to `Window/BBUnity/Animation/Animation Event Auditor`.
- New: **Movement Profile** window (Window > BBUnity > Generation): choose a platformer template and see its movement source (the player prefab's controller, or typed-in numbers) in cells — highest jump, longest jump, ledge heights and gap widths that work — with a drawing of the jump arcs. **Chunk Editor** window: paint a hand-authored chunk's cells (left button paints, right erases), place specific enemies, items and traps as markers, and mark edge sockets; chunk editing is exposed on `ChunkDefinition` (`Resize`, `SetCell`, `AddMarker`, `ToggleSocket`...). New `ChunkRoomLayoutStep` lays a level out from the template's chunk library (chunks become rooms of the chunk's room type, markers become placements) and `RoomLinking` is the shared code that joins rooms. `GenerationContext` now carries the template's `ChunkLibrary`.
- New: **Tile Palettes** window (Window > BBUnity > Tiles > Tile Palettes): create and edit tile palettes — the tilemap layers a level is drawn on (name, collision none / solid / one-way, sorting layer and order, physics layer) and, per cell type, the rule set that draws it and the layer it goes on. *Add Missing Cell Types* adds an entry for every cell type in the project, and a Checks section flags entries with no cell type or rule set, duplicate cell types, undefined layers and cell types nothing draws.
- New: **Tile Rule Editor** (Window > BBUnity > Tiles > Tile Rule Editor): lists every tile rule set in the project (search, create, rename, duplicate, delete) and edits the selected one — a wizard that generates its rules from a standard 13-tile layout (drag tiles into a grid of slots) or from a horizontal strip for one-way platforms; each rule as a clickable 3 x 3 neighbour grid (Any / Same / Different) with its weighted tiles; and a live preview on a test shape (click cells to add or remove ground, hover a tile to see which rule picked it). `TileRuleSetDefinition.FindRuleIndex` reports which rule matches.
- New: **Level Elements** and **Room Types** windows (Window > BBUnity > Generation): browse, search, create, rename, duplicate and delete level elements, element categories and room types without the Inspector. Elements are grouped and coloured by category, show a plain-language summary of their placement needs, warn when they have no prefab, and tick the room types they may appear in; categories and room types list what uses them. Both are built on a reusable `DefinitionBrowser` shell. Changed: **Install Platformer Defaults** moved to Window > BBUnity > Generation > Install (greyed out once a platformer template exists, and hidden in the Level Generator then) and now asks where to install — the suggested folder or one you choose.
- New: **generation editors** (`BBUnity.Editor.Generation`): **Window > BBUnity > Generation > Level Generator** — pick a template and seed, Generate, and inspect the result as a tile-free **schematic** (walls, rooms, connections, placed elements coloured by category, problems, and the cells the player can reach; scroll to zoom, drag to pan). Any parameter the run resolved can be pinned to a fixed value to test a case. *Build In Scene* turns the result into a `GeneratedLevelRoot` (prefab instances with undo, plus tilemaps if a tile palette is given) and *Clear Generated* removes it. Also: a recipe editor for generation templates (steps added from a type list and reordered, with type pickers for room placer / shape / connector), drawers for `IntParameter` / `FloatParameter` (Fixed or From-To), and **Window > BBUnity > Generation > Install Platformer Defaults**, which creates starter cell types, room types, element categories, player spawn and level exit (prefabs and elements) and a ready-to-run template in `Assets/BBUnity/Generation`. `GenerationResult` now records the parameters a run resolved; `RoomLayoutStep` can be configured in code; `PlatformerLevelTemplateDefinition.ApplyStandardRecipe` takes the parameters to use.
- New: **tiles** (`BBUnity.Tiles`): BBUnity's own tile rules on top of Unity's tile assets. A `TileRule` says what each of a tile's eight neighbours must be (Any / Same / Different) and which tile (or weighted variants) to use; a `TileRuleSetDefinition` holds the ordered rules (first match wins) and a fallback; `TileRuleResolver` is the pure logic that picks a tile for a grid cell (repeatable variants, level border optionally counted as solid); `TileRuleGenerator` builds a rule set from a standard 13-tile edge and corner layout (`EdgeCornerTileSet`) or a horizontal strip such as a one-way platform. A `TilePaletteDefinition` maps each `CellTypeDefinition` to a rule set and a tilemap layer, with `TilemapLayerSettings` (sorting, physics layer, none/solid/one-way collision); `TilemapPainter` paints a `GenerationGrid` into Tilemaps with merged composite colliders. `LevelBuilder.Build` takes an optional palette and adds the painted tilemaps under the generated level root.
- New: **level elements and the level builder** (`BBUnity.Gameplay.Levels.Elements`, `BBUnity.Gameplay.Levels.Generation`): `LevelElementDefinition` (a `PlaceableDefinition` that adds the prefab to instantiate) with `LevelElementCategoryDefinition`, the scene components `LevelElement`, `LevelRoom` and `GeneratedLevelRoot` (seed and template, so a level can be recreated), and `LevelBuilder`, which turns a `GenerationResult` into a `GeneratedLevelRoot` of room and element objects (through an `IElementInstantiator`, so editor and runtime share it). `Unity.BBUnity` now references `Unity.BBUnity.Generation`.
- New: **platformer generation** (`Unity.BBUnity.Generation.Platformer`, `BBUnity.Generation.Platformer.*`): `PlatformerMovementProfile` (jump, run, fall and body size in cells) with `MovementProfileSource` (read from a `PlatformingPlayerMovementController` prefab or entered by hand); `JumpSimulator` and `PlatformerReachability` (what the player can reach by walking and jumping); `PlatformerCorridorConnector` (floor-level passages with shafts between floor heights); `PlatformerRoomAssignmentStep` (start and exit rooms, spawn and exit placements), `PlatformerPlatformStep` (adds one-way stepping-stone platforms until every room is reachable) and `PlatformerValidationStep` (exit must be reachable; unreachable rooms and placements are warnings); `PlatformerGenerationParameters` and `PlatformerLevelTemplateDefinition`. `PlatformingPlayerMovementController` now exposes `JumpSpeed`, `AirborneGravity`, `MaximumFallSpeed` and `MaximumHorizontalSpeed`. `GenerationParameters` gained `DefaultSolid` so a template names its solid cell type once.
- New: **generation layout** (`BBUnity.Generation.Layout`): level layout is three pluggable parts — `RoomPlacer` (where rooms go), `RoomShape` (what a room looks like), `RoomConnector` (how rooms are joined) — combined by the general `RoomLayoutStep`, which links rooms with a minimum spanning tree plus optional loops (`ConnectionPlanner`). Defaults: `ScatterRoomPlacer`, `GridRoomPlacer`, `RectangleRoomShape`, `BlobRoomShape`, `LShapedCorridorConnector`, `WindingTunnelConnector`. `LayoutStep` is the base for whole-grid algorithms (first one: `CellularCaveLayoutStep`) and `RoomDetectionStep` turns open space into rooms and connections so population and the rest work on caves too. Includes `Carving` and `GridAnalysis` helpers.
- New: **generation foundations** (`Unity.BBUnity.Generation` assembly, `BBUnity.Generation.*`), the game-agnostic data layer for level generation: `GenerationGrid`/`GridLayer` with `CellTypeDefinition`, `GridRoom`/`RoomGraph`/`RoomTypeDefinition`, `IntParameter`/`FloatParameter` (Fixed or Range) with `ParameterOverrideSet`, `GenerationStep`/`GenerationPipeline`/`GenerationContext`/`GenerationResult` with a seeded `GenerationRandom`, hand-authored `ChunkDefinition`/`ChunkLibraryDefinition`, `PlaceableDefinition` with `PlacementCandidateFinder` and `PopulationStep`, and `GenerationTemplateDefinition` (runs at edit time or runtime). The platformer-specific generation, tile rules and editor windows build on this.
- New: **component management** in the Inspector, switched on with `BBUnitySettings` > Component Management > Enabled (off by default). Components are sorted into collapsible category sections under the GameObject header by the rules of a **preset**, chosen in the same settings section (the dropdown ends in *Create New Preset...*). BBUnity ships a built-in `BBUnity` preset (Physics, Rendering, Cinemachine, BBUnity Controllers); custom presets are `ComponentCategoryPresetDefinition` assets (`Assets/Editor/BBUnity/ComponentPresets`) managed in **Window > BBUnity > Component Presets**. A rule maps component types (including everything derived from them) or namespaces to a category; the first matching rule wins. Unity's own editors for sorted components are hidden with `ActiveEditorTracker.SetVisible`, so turning the feature off restores the normal Inspector. Nothing is stored on GameObjects. Needs Unity's internal `InspectorWindow` (found by name in `BBUnity.Editor.Support.ComponentManagement.InspectorWindows`); if a Unity upgrade changes it the tools switch themselves off with one warning.
- Removed the unused `CoyoteState` and `IEntityController`.

### Fixed

- `PlayerMovementController`: a jump press could be lost when the frame it landed on had no physics step. It's now latched until a physics step consumes it (`InputState.JumpRequested` / `ConsumeJumpRequest()`).
- `PlayerMovementController`: the extended slope-stick ground check no longer applies while rising, which kept the player "grounded" for a few frames after takeoff and let slope projection flatten the jump.
- `PlayerMovementController`: releasing jump while rising now reliably ends the jump early (latched until apex), and only for jump-initiated ascents. `ApplyMovement`'s parameters are renamed `jumpPressed` / `jumpHeld` (order unchanged).
- `BootLoaderController` no longer async-loads its Scenes To Load (Frontend) when the game was entered at a Level or Gameplay scene — only when entered at the Boot scene.
